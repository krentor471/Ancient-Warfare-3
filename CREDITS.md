# Ancient Warfare 2 Credits and Notices

This file preserves attribution carried by the Ancient Warfare 2 1.12.2 project and its bundled structure and skin library. Names are reproduced as they appeared in the legacy project. The 1.20.1 release is a modified, unofficial community remaster; the original authors are not responsible for defects introduced by this version.

## Project and Code

- Ancient Warfare 2 Remastered contributors: modernization, extensive rewrites, restored content, compatibility work, testing, and release maintenance for this modified version.

## Recreated Models

All model files used by the 1.20.1 remaster were newly recreated with AI-assisted tools and workflows. The purpose was to remove the legacy CodeChickenLib model-rendering dependency and keep a future Fabric version possible, because CodeChickenLib is not available for Fabric.

This model declaration does not apply to, supersede, or remove attribution for legacy textures, skins, structures, visual designs, or other contributed assets. See `MODEL_NOTICE.md` for the complete declaration.

## Structure Library Notice

The legacy structure credits identify Mohawky as the structure build-team leader and state that roughly half of the bundled structures are Mohawky's original work, granted for continuing use by the Ancient Warfare 2 mod. The library also contains structures created, donated, adapted, or used with permission from many other builders.

The legacy notice says that attribution may be incomplete. It also says that some publicly uploaded structures were included after their creators could no longer be contacted. No ownership of those builders' work is claimed here. A creator who finds missing or incorrect attribution, or requests removal of their work, should contact the maintainer of the distribution carrying this file.
